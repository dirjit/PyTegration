      Fun facts

        The Light Time Machine

        Light is the fastest thing there is — but space is so big that even light takes ages to cross it. When you look at a star, you see light that left it long ago. Every telescope is a time machine!

        Space Gym

        Your mass (how much "stuff" you're made of) is the same everywhere. Your weight is how hard a world's gravity pulls on you — and that changes!

            Walk the Solar System

            Shrink the Sun to the size of a ball and see where everything else would be. Pictures in books squash the planets together — the real Solar System is mostly empty space.

            Scroll the track sideways to take the walk. Planets are drawn much bigger than their true size — at real scale most would be smaller than one pixel!
