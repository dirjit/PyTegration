Two pulls. One sheet.

We own two drums.

That is the whole studio. Fluorescent pink and process blue. Everything on this page is those two inks pulled in a different order, at a different screen angle, on a different paper.

Fluorescent Pink

Drum one. 26 degree screen.

Process Blue

Drum two. 52 degree screen.

The overlap

No drum. Only where the two land together.

The second pull

Blue goes down first and dries. Then the sheet goes back in and pink is pulled over the top of it.

The purple is what happens in between. Scroll and you are running the second drum.

The purple is not a colour we chose. It is a colour that happens.

Blue last

The faults we keep.

  • Slip

    The sheet moves under the drum. The second colour lands a millimetre off the first.

  • Roller

    Bands of ink pulled along the sheet where the roller carried too much.

  • Ghosting

    A pale second impression of a heavy area, printed further down the same sheet.
