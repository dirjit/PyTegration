#   resolver such as `pip-compile` (pip-tools) or `uv pip compile`, which also
#   records transitive dependencies and cryptographic hashes (`--require-hashes`)
#   so a compromised mirror cannot swap a package out from under you. We keep the
#   list hand-curated here so the lesson stays readable.
